01 · What It Does

Pay Cash, or Finance & Invest the Difference?

Paying cash means no interest, no monthly payment. Financing frees that same cash to be invested instead — which path wins depends on rate, term and return. This tool walks a guest through it with their own numbers.

INPUTS

Guest-Driven

Investment amount, expected return (Conservative / Balanced / Growth / Aggressive presets), interest rate, and term in years.

OUTPUT

Live Comparison

Invest vs. Finance vs. Net Gain, side by side, recalculated instantly as numbers change.

Pulled out of the main guide so it can be shared and used on its own — a natural next step once a guest has picked their protection plan and the conversation turns to how to pay for it.
